Commiting to the Grind

I have been slowly finding my way again. Step by step, brick by brick, moving forward, building myself up. It is hard to explain how good it feels to be on the right track, one thousand times or more better than over indulging as I did before. It’s more difficult that’s for sure. I find myself reading labels again and with less choices in my purchases since changing to eating plant based. Waking up at 6am can be tough but not nearly as tough as going to bed at 22:00. Step by step, brick by brick. I am growing and proud to be improving. I will grind until the day I die, I will be the best version of me possible.
