In Case of Emergency

A reminder of the truth for when the mind sinks into darkness.

I know life seems harder right now. Everything seems pointless, even reading this seems pointless. Just remember you have been here before. Every time you beat it, you are a warrior. It’s times like these when you get to prove your merits. Now is when you earn your colours.

You are a miracle and you deserve love. Love yourself first, be kind.

Top Ten Steps to Find your Way

  1. Everything seems worse now, remind yourself what is good.
  2. Don’t listen to any negativity, if it’s not positive then shut it up.
  3. Give yourself a little time to rest. Slow down. Use this time to reinforce positive thinking.
  4. Read your goals, remember your focus.
  5. Stop thinking so far ahead, a journey of a 1000 miles starts with a single step.
  6. Take the time to take action in the areas you have been neglecting. Small steps.
  7. Meditate on love. You too deserve love.
  8. Reminisce about the good times, listen to old songs, look at old photos. Saudade. Without sadness, there can be no joy.
  9. Inspect your diet, get eating healthy.
  10. Start exercising. Small steps.

Love yourself first, and all others after.
