Overcorrecting My Social Life

I've been noticing that too much socialising drains me, while too little leaves me feeling isolated and lonely.

Today has been a hectic day. One of those that never seem to end, yet you don’t remember exactly how they started.

I have this tool that I use to do weekly check ins. It helps me stay on track and focus on the short term. It’s manually geared towards maintaining general balance and ensuring the short term is meeting my long term goals, over helping with the long term goals themselves.

As part of this process, this week I set one of my objectives to:

Send one low friction text to a new person

You see, I ranked my social life lowest of the 5 domains and realised I had been letting it slip. But I may have over corrected. It’s been a busy week; I have had a meet-up planned with one friend every day and some days two.

When I was younger, I found myself energised by being around people, but these days I notice that people drain me. I recharge when I am alone. Something changed in me over the years, I guess.

I am extremely grateful to have people to spend time with. And their company is a blessing. But I realise that I need some rest and some time alone to recoup.

Today I am finishing a challenge I started 30 days ago, but I am so tired that I’m not excited about it. I will celebrate tomorrow, I suppose.

I wanted to check in, because life has shown me recently that everything comes down to one thing:

You have to show up.

Catch you on the next one.
