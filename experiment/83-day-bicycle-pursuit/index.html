The 83-Day Bikepacking Adventure

Quit the job. Sell everything. 3000 km from London to Lisbon - by bicycle, in winter.

## The Setup

A corporate life that wasn't mine. Sell down to what a bicycle can carry, quit the job, and ride from London to Lisbon in winter. The question: how little can I need, and will getting there alone be enough to feel like I chose my own life?

## Protocol

  1. 01Sell everything non-essential and document what remains.
  2. 02Buy a second-hand bicycle and only the gear needed for a winter crossing.
  3. 03Map a rough London-to-Lisbon route with a daily distance baseline.
  4. 04Fix a departure date, then keep moving until the destination is reached.
