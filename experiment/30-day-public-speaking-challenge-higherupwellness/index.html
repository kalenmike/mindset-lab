Thirty days of output: recording a 60 second video every day for release to Instagram. #higherupwellnesschallenge

9 days remaining

## The Setup

Volume over polish. One 60-second video per day for 30 days. Stacking reps until the reps stop being scary.

## Protocol

  1. 01Record one 60-second talk on a daily.
  2. 02Schedule the video for posting to Instagram, no matter how cringe.
  3. 03No edits, no cuts.
  4. 04Review yesterday's output and pick one improvement.
  5. 05Log reps, reach, and energy each evening.
