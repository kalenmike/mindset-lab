## The Setup

Consistency has been my failing when it comes to meditation. And starting again always seems harder. So I'm linking my meditation to Yoga. The hope is that the movement will keep my mind calm enough to make it through the full 20 minutes, while still being able to enter a deep state of meditation.

## Protocol

  1. 01Meditate daily with Yoga.
  2. 02Session must be a minimum of 20 minutes.
  3. 03Movement can be sun salutations or any other type of yoga.
