## Hola! I am Kalen and this is my ~excuse to travel~ pursuit of **happiness, meaning and purpose.**

I woke up one day and realised that my life wasn't making me happy _(really it was a few months coming)_.  

Spontaneously I quit my job, sold almost everything, and in its place I bought a bicycle. I had no idea what I was getting myself into.

**Kickoff:** London, United Kingdom  
**Destination:** Lisbon, Portugal
