It all changed. Colours had drained and the future now seemed more distant and incoherent than it had ever been. The struggle is real and every simple action, every simple step now seems harder than climbing mount Everest. My brain is telling me that hunger is better than the effort required to make food. I want to cry but I cannot seem to muster enough sadness. I am sad, but it's as though the sadness has gone beyond its point of origin and has replaced itself with a void. Feelings have no place here and emotions that should drive me to tears reveal themselves as a lump in my throat. I feel heavy tension in my forehead and cheekbones. I’m unable to relax my neck.

My inner voices do battle with each other. One strong voice silently whispers, “There is no point in carrying on. You have come as far as you need come. Die, extinguish yourself.” While another weak and distant voice screams for survival. It reminds me to carry on, that things will get better. The sun will shine and I will once again find happiness. If I just put in the work.

Then the authority stands up. The oldest voice of them all and it says, “That’s all well and good, but you know you will feel this way again. Life is always going to be a struggle. Why struggle. You can see clearly now. It’s time to end this game.”

I’m able to force myself to take some small action but it musters most of my will power. And after I feel more drained than when I began. I know that exercise will help, but exercise seems pointless. I know that eating well will improve my feelings, but preparing food seems too hard. I feel alone despite being around other people. They don’t understand, how could they.

They look at me and see laziness. But I have felt laziness and this is not the same. They look at me and see apathy. But I care, I care too much. Without the care I would be long gone. I would have played out one of my many fantasies. I would have swam out into the ocean until tiredness took me away. I would have climbed the tallest structure only to take the quick way down. I would have plunged a knife deep into my abdomen. But I care, I care too much, and I fear pain.

There was a time not so long ago where I felt I could conquer the world. Nothing would stop me from reaching for what I wanted. Now I fear that I no longer want anything. And if I did, I am no longer worthy to receive it.

The only rest seems to come from sleep, and the hope that I will not awaken. Inside I wish to scream, but I don’t know what I want to scream about. I want to cry but I have no tears. I want to die but I have no courage.

I have climbed the highest mountains only to dive into the lowest seas. It is not the beasts and monsters of the outside world that plague me. I am not prevented from reaching success by any outside force. Instead my world has turned against me. My mind has become my enemy.

The colours around me are no longer bright. My future seems bleak. And should I not find my light I fear the light shall not find me. As irrational as this all seems. It's as real as the blood in my veins. Like a see saw I rock from wanting to live to wanting to die. In all this confusion I am able to discern that it is not death that I wish upon myself but rather the cessation of suffering.

And when all of life seems to bring more suffering how else do you set yourself free?

Today I will not die. Today I will not live. Today I merely exist waiting, waiting for it all to end.
