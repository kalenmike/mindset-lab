Reflected on the white sand were shimmers of light caused by the light movement of the water above. Perhaps it was the waters’ way of expressing the stars it had seen the night before. The water was so clear it was easy to see right to the bottom. The tiny fish that swam in large groups cast inch long shadows. Creating a depth not easy to imagine and yet so hard to forget.

The waves in the distant though calm lapped a starfish ashore. Unsure of its intention I returned this tropical creature to the sea. There was magic in the air and I did all I could to swallow the images with my eyes.

If perfection was a moment this would be it. Behind me just off the beach was a single tree that cast a shadow, a shelter from the sunlight. Underneath it sat my father, my stepmother, their dog who had inherited the role of my sister and the woman I had come to love in the recent weeks.

When we all got up to walk it was through the knee-high water. I pushed our dreaded dog along on the paddle board. It felt to me like we were voyagers, adventurers on an exciting discovery. And in some ways we were. We entered a secluded cove that had a natural beach. Above the water carved cave, we found the carvings of pirates that had been before, yet were now long gone.
