Illuminating the sky was a crescent moon. It shone more white than yellow on this dark night. The moonlight, for the most part, was the only light around for miles. We looked up at the stars just off to the right, and in that instance I couldn't help myself. I ran off to find a better view. 

After placing an old ladder under the rafters of what appeared to be a hurricane damaged roof I fetched her. She looked so beautiful as I approached. Her long blonde hair reflected just enough light to contrast with the sky around her. Peering out into the night sky her presence made my heart skip a beat. Before I knew it, we were sitting on the rafters, then she was edging her way up the steep roof tiles. I joined her. 

We lay there for what seemed like an eternity, sharing in our minds. We talked while the heavens blessed us and stars shot past us. Were I alone, I wouldn't have braved climbing from the rafters onto the roof. It was when she confessed she wouldn't have climbed the ladder to rafters without me that I knew. I knew then that I had found someone special and that together we would achieve anything.
