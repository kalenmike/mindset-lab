The visions shared for the last 2.5 months evaporate instantly and in their place I see a sudden shift in colors. A single bed lies in front of me,  a strange bed. The size and shape look new to me.

Feelings begin to rise up bringing pain of the looming departure. I find myself unsure whether I have been here before despite this being the very same bed I have been using my whole stay.

In this moment it all becomes clear to me. Sounds are louder, colors are brighter and my heart holds within it a light pain. The kind of pain you feel just before an untimely breakup. Or just before you part from those you love dearly unsure when you will once again share in each other's spirit. 

**It's a strange thing that the very fact that something can, or even will end, is what makes you wish it won't.**
