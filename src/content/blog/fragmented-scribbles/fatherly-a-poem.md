Waking Early, Zombie Walks  
Kitchen Refills, Work Drive Talks  
Birds chirp faster, Cool wind blows  
Sunlight showers, Darkness exposed  

Father teaches, his son learns  
Life; it's lessons, its concerns  
Moments passing, time shared well  
Of these moments, son will tell  

Love you Father, Love you Son  
Words not expressed with the morning tongue  
Moments passing, future comes  
Forget not today, live as one
