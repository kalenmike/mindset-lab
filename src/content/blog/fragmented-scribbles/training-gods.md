If every being on this planet is a reincarnation of me. My only goal is to remind all beings I encounter to love. This should be done by example and not forcefully. All judgments should be forgotten, all resentments forgiven. Because there will be a time when I will meet the current version of me, and I have the choice now to make that a pleasant experience that will guide me in the right direction.
