A friend recently mentioned to me a problem she was facing. A friend of hers had pointed out a fault she was being bothered by and did so in a way that was hurtful. Her friend had mentioned that it had been discussed when she was not around. This left her feeling as though her friends were against her and left her doubting the value of her friendships. She was also worrying about who may have been involved in this talking behind her back. Perhaps you have found yourself in a similar situation?

The first thing I constantly remind myself is:
What people say about me when I'm not around is NONE OF MY BUSINESS! Don't get caught up in gossip, even if that gossip involves you. Everyone is entitled to their own personal views. Often people will talk about something or someone only to vent the issue and in doing so perhaps realize it was not as extreme as they had originally thought.
If the matter is serious they will bring it up to you in time. Although those around you the freedom to express their emotions without being offended. Even if the topic is sometimes you.

If an issue is eventually raised to you remember this:
A constructive criticism is not a criticism of you as a person but a small action or habit you have. Do not make a mountain out of a molehill. We often add our own baggage to the words of others completely distorting the meaning and intentions.

Take it at face value! A door is a door and a window is a window. Stop telling yourself stories!!
