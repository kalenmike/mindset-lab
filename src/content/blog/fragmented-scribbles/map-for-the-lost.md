When you find yourself lost in a difficult situation this may help. I was given some advice by a friend I once knew in Spain.

> “When you find yourself in the ditch of the road, stop, close your eyes and look at your star. Decide what you want most in life and go for it with your head and your heart together. Come On!”

I think in this day and age many of us find ourselves lost and confused, with all we have gained we seem to have lost something. An understanding, a connection, some feeling that we are missing something persists. With all our choice it is easy to fill this void temporarily, with alcohol, drugs, artificial connection.

I read an interesting study the other day regarding addiction. By creating a mouse utopia and offering the mice a choice of regular water and cocaine spiked water, the aim was to test the relationship between addiction and connection. What they found was that mice in a happy environment used the drug in very small doses, and some not at all. In comparison isolated mice abused the drug until it induced death. Their findings made them insist that the opposite of addiction was not sobriety, but instead human connection, relationships.

Perhaps we have isolated ourselves from each other. We have been taught to fit in and do what is expected by others. There is a way to read, a way to write, a way to play the guitar, and even a way to pick up girls. We follow the advice of others who have been before us, but even more than that, we believe that it is the only way to progress. Constantly failing to remember that at some point even what is now considered standard was created, invented, established. In all likelihood there exists a better way to do everything we now conceive of.

Decide for yourself, what is the purpose of life. Is it financial success? Superiority in one form or the other? Or perhaps it is simply to live. So I say to you my friends; if you find yourselves lost, look at your star, decide what you want most, and go for it. With your head and your heart together!
