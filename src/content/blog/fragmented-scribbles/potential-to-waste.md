Despite the usual warmth emitted from deep brown mahogany, the table glared a coldness I had not seen before from wood. It was as though it was still alive and reflected its feelings of isolation. 

Resting in the middle of the table was a shiny silver pen. Its tip flared out and sharpened to a point like an unusual arrowhead. It lay there alone and no inkwell was in sight. While it had the ability to make a mark on the world, it could not do so without the ink it required. More importantly the pen depended upon someone to shape its movements and design its markings. 

Directly below was a crisp white rectangle of paper. If you focused your eyes it was easy to make out the shallow depressions as the light danced around it. How would this paper complete its purpose without an animated object to mark it? As I looked at the scene in front of me it became clear that despite its potential nothing could change. One crucial ingredient had been missing. **Without desire, action and movement these objects would lie here forever slowly decomposing.** Until finally the potential they once showed no longer existed.
