Confidence building. The art of becoming confident. It’s a funny thing to consider. In fact, most of us do not believe we are good enough. Even the vast majority of those who will say they do believe it are still held back by various limiting beliefs: 

<ul>
    <li>The fear of missing out.</li>
    <li>The fear of not being good enough.</li>
    <li>The fear of approaching someone they find attractive.</li>
    <li>The fear of being laughed at.</li>
    <li>The fear of being judged.</li>
</ul>

I once heard it said that: 

> **"Fear is an acronym for <u>F</u>alse <u>E</u>vidence <u>A</u>ppearing <u>R</u>eal."**

In many ways this makes sense. If you think about it, fear always comes before anything bad actually happens. And before something happens there is always an equal and just as likely opportunity that something good will happen.

Ask yourself why do we always assume the worst? If it is our evolutionary tact. A survival mechanism that keeps us from perishing and if so does it also keep us from truly living? I wonder how long it would take to begin truly replacing fear with confidence. To make the first thought that enters our brains a good one.

To truly wish ourselves the best in every day and moment!
