At times in life we can get so caught up in trying to achieve something that we completely lose track of the reason we started in the first place. We work so hard at something day by day that we begin to believe it is what we are working on that is necessary. It almost comes to define us.

For that reason I believe we should, at regular intervals, stop and review our lifestyle choices and inspect whether we are in fact heading in the right direction.

**Fitness**  
To become stronger and more able. To move freely without restriction. To feel energized and alive. I use these activities to accomplish this: calisthenics, yoga, and dance.

**Health**  
To be healthy. To not experience undue pain. To operate at an optimal level. For this I eat a whole food diet 95% of the time. I also practice Pranayama.

**Money**  
To have enough money that it no longer becomes a concern. To free my mind to think about other things than money. To free my time to concentrate on what is important to me regardless of how much it pays. I am building a brand to accomplish this.

**Intelligence/Mindset**  
To have a mind that promotes positive actions. To find the good in all situations. To never limit myself due to incorrect thoughts. To find new ways to grow. For this I read self-help books and practice affirmations.

**Spirituality**  
To be at peace with the Earth. To feel compassion and love for all things. To not allow myself to feel insignificant or to belittle others. I practice gratitude writing, Buddhism and veganism.

**Skills**  
To acquire useful skills that aid me on my journey. To build confidence in myself and my abilities. To feel as though I have the tools needed to live the life I want. I practice the bo-staff, speaking from my diaphragm, selling successfully, and correct posture.

**For all things in life have a reason and a purpose. Yet to lose sight of this is a great loss indeed. How can one pursue their dreams when they lack the direction they are meant to proceed.**
