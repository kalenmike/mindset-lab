Trying to find our place in this world is anything but easy. So many options, so many choices. I feel that sometimes it's impossible to succeed. Should I follow my heart or the opinion of others. As if tied into something I don't quite understand. I can see the ropes I'm expected to climb, but I'm not sure where they will take me, or which one I should choose.

I was told once that life has a plan for us, that we have a destiny. I should simply give into the greater goodness. Not concentrate, without planning. Just follow the signs. That if I gave into everything that was around me I would always be in that place where I was supposed to be. I have heard many stories about positive thinking or even watched movies with the likes of 'The Secret' and reviewed their philosophies. Even perhaps experimented. By visualizing I have often met success. I have accomplished that which I believed impossible. I have achieved that which I believed un-achievable.

Perhaps it was the mere fact that I never finished what I started, I never managed to succeed that brought me to where I am now. To this frame of mind, this way of thinking. As if a dream had overcome me, I merely followed what was there for me to see, for me to find. The great adventure of a lifetime, the most exciting treasure hunt. The search for self discovery, the search for ideals, for belief, for recognition, for understanding, for truth, and for a future.

The grass was long, thick to the touch, it almost felt hard and dry, yet I could see the life inside it, the vibrant green that it held. As I walked over it I could feel the earth and a shiver ran up my spine. The sky was the most amazing blue, I never knew that such a colour existed and for a moment I contemplated its existence. I would often spend days concocting brainteasers or questioning reality and one of my favourite thoughts was about colour. I would ask myself was my red the same colour that everyone else saw, and if our perception of colour was different what effect did that have on humanity? After all colour is used to describe moods, emotional states, good and evil. One colour can say a thousand things, yet we have no way to share the colour that we see. We have just been taught to call it by a name.

This was one of the hottest days we had in a long time. The air was just the right humidity that the heat was pleasant, and I had a great contentment just being outside, just sharing in this day. My skin was pale, I don't remember turning this white, I looked lifeless, sickly. I felt tired. I had not been outside for a long time. A very long time. I felt as if my chains had been broken, as I closed my eyes for a second there was a burst of wind, it was refreshing and it shifted my hair out of my face, for that moment I was flying. On the wings of angels I soared the skies. Upon opening my eyes **I felt that shiver again rising up my spine, concentrating on the top of my head, forcing a smile onto my face.**

The sun reflected onto the shed window in such a way that it turned it into a mirror, I could see my face and as I walked up to it I could see my long black hair, it was dirty and accentuated the black lines under my eyes. I was so pale. I remember when the green in my eyes showed everyone that I was alive, I would change the world. I could not see that light anymore, the green was now dull. How did I let this happen to me, how long had I been lost. With a thousand thoughts running through my mind I felt overwhelmed. I had to take this one step at a time. I had to piece this together bit by bit. This was going to take me awhile. I should eat.
