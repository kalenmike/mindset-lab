Sometimes in life we find ourselves unsure of the next best step. Heck sometimes we don’t even know what to do next, let alone what is best. Plan as you may, sometimes life will blindside you. You are going to wake up at some point and be completely clueless about your direction.

When this happens. When you find yourself staring down a long empty road entirely unsure of what to do next. Remember “shit happens”.

Progress is not a straight line, instead it comes in intervals. In between every improvement you spend a lot of time just being mediocre. Grinding. All the best things in life come from hard work. Boring work. Waking up early work. Blood, sweat and tears, work. It's never too late to start putting in the work. Just remind yourself what it is you are striving for. And when times get hard, when you get lost. When there is no clear way forward. When you are lost in the jungle. When the waves are threatening to sink your ship. When no one is hearing you cry. When you are abandoned. When the world seems empty and you seem lost. That’s when it really counts. Everything before was practice, now you are in the game. This is when the moment calls to prove what you have. Prove what you can do. It's always tougher, always harder when it counts. Practice is preparation. Game time is game time. Will you break or will you make it?

So, what do you do when you have no idea what to do? List your choices, compare them with your ultimate goals and then choose the one that fits best. You only get one choice. Then do that, with everything you have, no matter how hard it feels. Push harder. Get yours.
