## The Weight of it All

First, let me fill you in on the current situation.

I'm based in Valencia, Spain. Recently single, going through the hardest year of my life.

Its not only that my relationship ended, 
or that I got caught up being an unexpected dad to 5 guinea pigs. 

Its not only that I lost my dad right before New Years, 
or that the funeral preparations were an absolute disaster full of opportunists,
or that the circumstances around his death were extremely suspicious.

Its not only that my sister decided to stop speaking to me during all of this, 
or that I stopped eating and lost 10kg of muscle.

Its not only that my one best friend left the city,
or that my savings are running low,
or that AI has shaken up my career landscape.

But the worst of it all is that I lost my confidence and my own mind turned against me. For a while anyway.

## Putting Up a Fight

The more life beat me, the less effect it seemed to have. I began to realise that at the end of all of this I too would end up taking the long cold nap and nothing that happened today was really as serious as that.

And my dad always told me:

> "Never give up"

So I summoned what was left inside of me and started to dig my way back out.

### Hitting the Pavement

I got back into running and found a release from the stress.

<Polaroid
    image={kalenRunning}
    caption="Running along the harbor."
/>

I often hoped a day would come that I would spend mornings running on the beach with my dad. That day never came. Yet despite him being gone I feel him running with me now, _even though he hated running_.

In the last 18 weeks I went from the couch to my first half marathon. And now I've started training for my first full one. I'm at 26 of 42 km so far.


### Sunrises and Sea Water

I've also been running a [community](https://www.instagram.com/valenciaseaswims/) with some friends that meets every Tuesday to watch the sunrise from the ocean water.

I can't say its been easy, many Tuesday mornings I wished I could avoid it. But I'm happy I didn't, it's kept me at least a little sane through this absolute ringer of a year.

### Connecting with Something Greater

I found God again, _or perhaps God found me_. Not in the traditional religious sense. I don't find myself aligning with Christianity, or Catholicism, or Islam. I just feel connected with this idea that there is something greater and grander than what we are. Something that many call God, but then go on the define in different ways.

I've been talking to Him. Very relaxed like, as if speaking with an old friend, and it's been helping me. I also talk to my dad. Both are one way conversations.

### Building an Escape Hatch

I've been putting in work to grow on social media, rather unsuccessfully I might add. Most days I consider quitting, but then I remind myself of the internal growth that showing up has given me.

Facing the embarrassment of failing in public, and accepting the sting of working on something you are proud of. Only for it to receive no recognition.

Its been a whirlwind of an adventure. From not caring, to caring, to caring too much, to realising its only ever been about creating to being with.

### One Step in Front of the Other

For the most part, life is a mixture of hope and fear. Hope, for the positive change that I can see in the light at the end of this tunnel. Fear, for the possibility that I am wrong about that light and it just turns out to be more darkness.

Regardless I carry on daily. Because I have come to realise that **when you don't know what to do just put one foot in front of the other and hope that your legs take you to a better place.**

## The Path Forward

During this process I stumbled across my old writings. Blogs, short stories, and poems. All outlets that I used to value once upon a time. 

After reading and feeling the past through them I've decided to return to the world of pen to paper, or finger to key. And as testimony to that, this is my first addition to my new collection of writings. Because even if nobody ever reads this, I may stumble across it in the future and remember what it was like to be here today. 

And be thankful that I made it through it all.
