Since putting my writing back online I have noticed an odd silence. This site is static, and it has no integrated feedback loop. It has made me realise that I have grown accustomed to the reactions and comments provided by modern platforms.

## Identifying a Conflict

My workflow has been to create, then upload, then obsessively check the reactions. And if my art performed poorly, it would affect my emotional state. Making me want to quit, or worse still, hide away.

Publishing something here leaves me with no feedback, no little hearts of recognition, no comments to satisfy or destroy my ego. Just silence. The act of posting completes the work of creation.

Its oddly satisfying, almost like sitting alone at the beach listening to the waves roll in. Or rolling up the tent door from a mountain top to witness the birds dancing at sunrise.

## Attention Cravings

Even so part of me craves that feedback loop. I want to know if people are seeing me. I want to know what they think about what I have to say. I find the option of accepting feedback practical. It helps to improve and to motivate. 

Although, if I am being honest, those wants also assume that feedback is positive.

## Silent Relief

A different part of me finds relief in the silence. The opinions of others have no control over the value I place on my art. I can create freely. I can love the process, and I can love the results. Then I can release it, no feedback, no outside influence to affect my emotional stability. Only my opinion and my happiness for having created something.

I find posting into silence romantic and freeing. It lets me create as if in private while still sharing a piece of me with an unknown and possibly non-existent audience. Instead of holding the work open for reaction, it marks the end of the creation process. The posting becomes an act of release.

## So What Now

As I write this, I am trying to decide which of these two paths will serve me better long term. Should I implement reactions and comments or should I embrace the silence?

I don't have the answer today. So for now I will do nothing and continue to relish the space that comes after hitting upload.
