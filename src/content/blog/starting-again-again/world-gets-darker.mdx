Momentum has slowed down and what was easy is beginning to seem a little more difficult. My iron will power has begun to chip and it shows signs of being hollow. I question myself what could be bringing this about. I sleep more, much more. Unaware whether I am tired or whether I am escaping a short rest turns into a several hour sleep. I remind myself of the cycle of my life.

Could it be my diet that is causing these changes as it has definitely begun to falter. Perhaps it is the mental changes as my brain begins to consolidate the ideas of religion into my ones agnostic paradigms. **I try not to cling and remind myself that this too will pass.**
