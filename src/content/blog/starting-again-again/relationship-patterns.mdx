Here I am, coming out of my second relationship in 8 months. I jumped straight from one into another and followed the same patterns. It’s clear to me that I need to do some serious work on myself before I undertake another one. I did some research and I am showing signs of emotional immaturity. So here it goes. Here are the things you should watch out for next time you enter a relationship.

1. Don’t fall to early, give the relationship time to grow. Chances are if you fall to early it's not real attraction but the fulfillment of some unfulfilled need.
2. Don’t break up too soon. Relationships are hard work. If you want to break up every time things get hard, then your not ready to be in one.
3. Don’t be needy. A healthy person realises that everyone needs their space. Make sure not to demand too much time. Be available not demanding.
4. Make arguments productive. If an argument is not going to be productive there is really no need to have it, and if you have many unproductive arguments it might be time to look inward. It takes two to argue.

So today I am starting fresh. The rebirth of Kalen Michael. It’s difficult for me to look back at the last year and how far I fell from my ideal self. At the time it seemed like I would never fall this low again. Yet here I am, making the same mistakes, living the same poor lifestyle choices. It’s ironic. But today it changes. Today I am starting fresh, the new me will become what the old me once was, only better. I woke up at 6am and went for a run, then I had a meditation session. At 9am I will head out to meet Alan, my business partner, so we can make some sales. Today I get back on track, I am going to forget about women for awhile until I am strong enough to be who I need to be.
