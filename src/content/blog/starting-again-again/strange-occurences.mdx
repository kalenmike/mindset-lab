I find myself in an interesting place at the moment. With my recent efforts towards self-improvement and realisation I had begun to neglect my meditation. Seeking to replace it with the time spent in Yoga and Pranayama. I completed my first round of chosen guide books which I had categorised as follows:
The 4 hour workweek by Tim Ferris - Freedom
Think and Grow Rich by Napoleon Hill - Wealth
How to Win Friends and Influence People by Dale Carnegie - Relationships
Psycho-Cybernetics by Max Maxwell - Mindset
At the time of completion I did not think much of this and decided to read an unrelated book before starting my second round. While eating lunch, I was waiting for a YouTube video to load and an advert appeared that introduced me to Autobiography of a Yogi by Yogananda. The advert had such an effect on me that I began to investigate more about the book. On discovering that many prominent people had read this book, some many times, I decided to consider buying it. Find the book for £1.50 I promptly purchased it.

I have been deeply drawn to the book and reading it has been very easy even passing around two hours the second day of reading. Strangely I began to feel that this book should be added to my reading round as the Spiritual book. I imagined that it was the glue that held the others together. Interestingly there are many ideas that are shared with the other books of my reading round.

When the Vedic was mentioned I recalled my earlier friend explaining his vedic astrology to me. At the time I didn’t listen much as I found astrology to be a topic not worth my time. This book sparked my interest and I decided to get my chart done. After reading my chart I felt devoid of energy and took a long nap. When I eventually woke up I was urged to continue reading the book which began promptly by explaining astrology and our ability to change what was written.

**I am unsure what road I am being taken down but I believe myself to be in the right place.**
