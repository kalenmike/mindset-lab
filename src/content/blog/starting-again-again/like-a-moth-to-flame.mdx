Recently I have fallen short of my goals. My to do lists vanished for about a month and I found myself doing much less than normal. My exercise routine dropped to once per week, yoga became erratic, and I haven’t read much in the last month. Depression was closely shadowing me, and whether the lack of goalkeeping was causing the depression or the depression was causing the lack of goalkeeping, I found myself achieving less than expected.  My diet suffered too as meals became less regular and I ate out more often.

I spent the last 3 days in bed recovering from a cold which gave me time to think and internalise something. This has given me the courage to begin building myself back up to where I was two months ago. **This entry is to serve as a reminder that sometimes we fall down, but we can always get back up again!**
