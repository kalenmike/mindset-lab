It’s a funny thing life. The consistency of the battle that is. It seems that no matter what I do to get myself back up, I eventually find myself down again. Down and questioning but what is the point. But are we asking ourselves the right questions?

Is there truly a point to life. Inevitably we are all going to leave this plane of existence, one way or another depending on your beliefs. Nothing we collect in this life will come with us, perhaps not even our memories and experiences. Its as if our entire existence will be wiped clean on our deathbeds, or at the very least once the last person on earth remembers our name.

If we cannot control this common fact, what can we control? Perhaps only the years in between. The dash on your gravestone. Surely we have control over this. However, many of us find ourselves continuing to live an existence that does not fulfill us. Doing things purely to help the clock tick, living as if we will live forever.

What would over lives mean if we seized every opportunity, lived every moment, smiled at every stranger. What unbelievable tap would we open, what abundance would come pouring into our lives.

For just 1 week witness the sunrise and the sunset everyday and see how it changes you!
