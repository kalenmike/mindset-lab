Recently I have felt uneasy about the goals I set myself some months ago. I decided that it was time to consolidate the goals I have set into my life long purpose. It is possible this will change over the coming months, years, or decades. Yet with this new purpose I feel at ease.

“I want for nothing. Everything I need and want is made available for me at the right moment.
In every situation, I offer the best of myself. I give my best of me to everyone I meet. Guided by meditation and self-discovery I give through my heart, my soul and my passion. Everyday and in everyway, I work towards my goals. I never give up. I connect people. I help them to express their inner identities by shining a light on the goodness. I remind people of their inner beauty and of their potential, in the process, I discovered my own. 
I train the Mind and the Body. Mind is improved through meditation, reading, affirmations and correct mindset. Body is improved through healthy vegan nutrition, calisthenics and yoga. Body and Mind are jointly trained with cold showers, minimalism, and no fap.
I live a life of love and compassion. I am love.
And so it is!”
