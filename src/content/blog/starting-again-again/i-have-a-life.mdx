It's a strange thing looking back at days past. I’ve been going through my old photos clearing up the ones that mean nothing in an attempt to create an album that I will actually use. Some memories are good and make me happy, but others bring sadness. People I have lost, people who are still alive but we no longer speak. Some of them we just lost contact because of time, others hearts I broke, or perhaps my drunken behaviour forced them to leave. I know that I can never go back, that changing the past is not possible and the only step forward is to learn from it. The sadness and pain of longing makes me crave a cigarette, or some beer. Anything to take away the feeling that hits me at this moment. How I would love to share my love with those that used to exist in my life in days past. I hope that one day we meet again, in a new world with forgiveness in our hearts ready to start a new. I miss you all.
