I find myself in an interesting position. It has been many years since I  have considered devotion to a God. I have been agnostic and even vengeful to the idea of a God. My mind is tangled with confusion as it attempts to understand what it happening. My quest for worldly fulfillment has brought me full course back to spirituality. I find many ideas easy to accept as my Self-Development journey express many of the same concepts. **Could it be that spirituality is the glue that holds it all together?**

I do not feel this to be a passing fad. It is becoming clear that I am being called to religion, to spirituality, to God. With each passing moment I can feel the pull becoming stronger along with my desire to dedicate myself to a life of study and meditation. My struggle is in connecting my worldly ambitions with those of the divine.

It will be interesting to watch as this path unfolds, the discovery is thrilling. Who will I be at the next intersection of my life? The question both excites and scares me. This is a profound change in my beliefs and attitudes. This will surely redirect the course of my life. I find myself praying to the Divine Mother for guidance, an act I would not have even entertained in the not so distant past. **“Free me from the illusion of this life, do with me as you will.”**
