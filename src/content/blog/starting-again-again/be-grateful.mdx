I have had a very interesting last 3 months. It has become clear that I have not been making the best decisions in my life and I am now left dealing with the repercussions of these choices. My biggest challenge of late has been trying to get myself balanced. I regularly find myself challenged and the thought crosses my mind to give up and to move towards the victim mentality. It was a little bit of a coping mechanism in the past and the challenge is now to break the habit. I am pushing myself to be grateful for the things that I do have, as well as reminding myself that even without anything just being alive is a blessing. Life comes with many experiences, some good, some bad, but none of which you could experience if you were not alive. I have plans for the future, I have some ideas and I am taking steps to reach them. The path is not clear as of yet, but I am working on it everyday and trying to ensure that my decisions are leading me where I intend to go. I remember thinking in a past moment that it would be interesting to see where life will be taking me. I find myself thinking the same again, as though I have finally awoken from a long nap and I am now ready to take action. One thing I have noticed is that the hard times in my life tend to attract me towards comfort. The more stress I am experiencing the more I want to indulge in things that I normally would believe to not be great for me. It is a fine line that we walk in this life, and it seems that at any moment we could vere off into another direction. For this reason I remain diligent and hopeful of the future.
