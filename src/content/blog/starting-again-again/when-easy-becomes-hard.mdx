I’m approaching two months in Madrid. It’s been an interesting experience and I have come much further than I thought I would. I remember sitting in Turks and Caicos thinking I would never make it here in Spain. Focusing on all the potential things that would go wrong.

What’s strange is that my attitude has now shifted and now I find myself without any desire to return. I look back on my time in the Caribbean with an overall distaste. Despite all the beauty, it offered and the rescue from loneliness I was becoming someone other than me.

It took the spontaneous commitment to leave to turn that around. I felt myself returning almost as soon as I landed in Madrid airport. My first few weeks were spent very much idly hoping for the best. I took some action towards finding work and maintaining my mood. Yet mostly I just socialised and experienced my time in Spain.

Slowly the momentum built up and I found myself exercising daily and focusing more on my goals. After finding work and a room to rent the stability ensured I could focus more on self-improvement. I committed myself to read, practice yoga and meditate. These three activities became part of my morning routine and I did them as soon as I woke up every day. It was easy. Along with these I continued to exercise daily and only seemed to slack off on the occasional leg day. Leg workouts do not really excite me much, it is the exercises themselves that do not offer much in the way of excitement.

Over time the new and exciting exercises became normal and habit's formed. Unfortunately with this came complacency. It became easier to take days off. This was the usual occurrence in my life. I would push myself hard for a period of time only to slowly revert to how things were before.

Not this time. This time I accept the challenge. Is it harder to continue?! At the moment, yes, I find it more difficult to stick to my goals. Yet that will not prevent me from giving it all I have. I will continue to push forward with the challenges I have set for myself.

I wanted to record this event as this is the exact moment in time I would normally give up. I would feel that I deserve a break and that break would become permanent. I have done this countless times in my life and I refuse to continue repeating the same life over and over again.

Today I declare that easy has become hard, and that hard will not break me. I will keep pushing on. I will keep working hard. I will succeed in my endeavours for I am the protagonist of my story. I create the life I want.

Often when we find something becoming difficult it is because we have forgotten to break down the task into smaller segments. Chunking is the way forward. It only takes one step to start a journey and another one step continues the very same.

I will conquer this challenge. Whether it takes me a day or a lifetime!
